Ivo wanted the token before the other diggers came down the hill, so he was on the rope while the spoil tips were still grey. The well took its buckets one man at a time. If Pell or the Sutton brothers got to the lip first, he would stand in the cold until noon with his hands in his armpits, and the token would be as warm for them as it was for him.

The bucket was twenty fathoms down when the rope went slack. He braced a boot on the iron lip and waited for the weight to come back. Below him the tithe bell rang once, low and slow, and he counted the stroke out loud because there was nobody to hear it.

One. That was his.

He hauled the rope in hand over hand, as fast as his split knuckles let him. The bucket came up with an inch of black water in the bottom and a slate token lying in it, grey on one side and red-brown on the other where the iron had stained it. He closed his fist on it and felt the heat go up his arm, and the number settled behind his eyes where the numbers always went.

```
[TITHE PAID]
Level ........ 2 → 3
Held by well . 1
```

Three. He said it again under his breath to check it. One level short of copper, and a third of this one already down the shaft, held for him until harvest.

The bell rang a second time while he was still coiling the rope. He looked down the shaft. Nobody else was on the lip, and nobody had dropped a bucket.

He took the token across the yard to the counting table under the lean-to. Sabine Rook had the ledger open and the lamp lit, though the sky was light enough to read by. She held out her hand for the token without looking up.

"Level three," he said. "The rate's a third."

"I know the rate." She pressed the slate to the page and watched the ink move. "Second bell was yours too?"

"There was nobody on the rope."

She wrote the entry, blotted it, and turned the ledger over to the back. The pages there had red rules instead of blue, and a column of names in a hand that was not hers. He had never seen them open before. Sabine ran the pen down the column, slowly, and stopped it at his name.
